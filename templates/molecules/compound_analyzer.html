Solubility Analysis (ESOL Method)

ESOL Formula: LogS = 0.16 - 0.63×LogP - 0.0062×MW + 0.066×RB - 0.74×AP

Where MW = Molecular Weight, RB = Rotatable Bonds, AP = Aromatic Proportion. This model provides aqueous solubility estimation without requiring melting point data.

Toxicity Screening

Filters Used:

  • PAINS (Pan Assay INterference compoundS): Identifies compounds that may interfere with biological assays
  • Brenk Filter: Screens for known toxic or reactive functional groups (mutagenic, carcinogenic, unstable)

Lipinski's Rule of 5

Rules for Oral Bioavailability:

  • Molecular Weight ≤ 500 Da
  • LogP ≤ 5 (lipophilicity)
  • Hydrogen Bond Donors ≤ 5
  • Hydrogen Bond Acceptors ≤ 10
